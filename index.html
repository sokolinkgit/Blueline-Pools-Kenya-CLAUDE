Questions we hear often

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical pool build take?

Most residential pools take 6–10 weeks from groundbreaking to handover, depending on size and design complexity. Commercial waterparks and large hotel pools typically run 3–6 months. You'll get an exact timeline in your written quote.

Do you work outside Nairobi?

Yes — we serve all 47 counties in Kenya. Our teams have completed projects on the coast, in the Rift Valley, and in upcountry towns, with travel and logistics built into the quote.

Can you renovate an existing pool instead of a new build?

Absolutely. Cracked shells, failed filtration, outdated tiling and leaks are some of our most common renovation jobs — often finished faster and at a lower cost than starting from scratch.

Is maintenance included after the project is done?

Every build comes with a written workmanship warranty, and we offer ongoing maintenance contracts covering filtration, water treatment and equipment servicing for pools, saunas and solar heaters.

How do I get an accurate quote?

Send your location, rough size and the service you need through the contact form below, or message us directly on WhatsApp. We'll follow up to schedule a free site visit and prepare a fixed quote.
